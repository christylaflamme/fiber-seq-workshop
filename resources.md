# Resources

## Core papers
- **Fiber-seq.** Stergachis AB, Debo BM, Haugen E, Churchman LS, Stamatoyannopoulos JA. Single-molecule regulatory architectures captured by chromatin fiber sequencing. *Science* 368, 1449–1454 (2020). [doi:10.1126/science.aaz1646](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aaz1646)
- **fibertools (m6A calling and Fiber-seq BAM tools).** Jha A, et al. DNA-m6A calling and integrated long-read epigenetic and genetic analysis with fibertools. *Genome Research* 34, 1976–1986 (2024). [doi:10.1101/gr.279095.124](https://doi.org/10.1101/gr.279095.124)
- **FIRE (regulatory elements, haplotype-selective accessibility).** Vollger MR, Swanson EG, et al., Stergachis AB. A haplotype-resolved view of human gene regulation. *bioRxiv* (2024). [doi:10.1101/2024.06.14.599122](https://doi.org/10.1101/2024.06.14.599122)

## Related methods
- **DAF-seq** (a deaminase marks accessible DNA, single-molecule and single-cell). Swanson EG, et al., Stergachis AB. Mapping single-cell diploid chromatin fiber architectures using DAF-seq. *Nature Biotechnology* (2025). [doi:10.1038/s41587-025-02914-3](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41587-025-02914-3). Preprint: [doi:10.1101/2024.11.06.622310](https://doi.org/10.1101/2024.11.06.622310)
- **FiberFold** (predicts haplotype-specific 3D contact maps from Fiber-seq). Dubocanin D, et al., Altemose N. Integrating single-molecule sequencing and deep learning to predict haplotype-specific 3D chromatin organization in a Mendelian condition. *bioRxiv* (2025). [doi:10.1101/2025.02.26.640261](https://doi.org/10.1101/2025.02.26.640261) · [github.com/altemoselab/fiberFold](https://github.com/altemoselab/fiberFold)

## Biology behind the demo stops
- *XIST* is expressed only from the inactive X: Brown CJ, et al. *Nature* 349, 38–44 (1991). [doi:10.1038/349038a0](https://doi.org/10.1038/349038a0)
- X-inactivation escape genes, including *KDM6A*: Tukiainen T, et al. Landscape of X chromosome inactivation across human tissues. *Nature* 550, 244–248 (2017). [doi:10.1038/nature24265](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature24265)
- KvDMR1 / *KCNQ1OT1* (maternally methylated; antisense transcript from the paternal allele): Smilinich NJ, et al. *PNAS* 96, 8064–8069 (1999). [doi:10.1073/pnas.96.14.8064](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.96.14.8064)
